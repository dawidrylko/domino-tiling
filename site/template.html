Domino Tiling

GitHub Blog post Dawid Ryłko

How many ways are there to cover a 2n × 2n board with pieces that each cover two neighbouring cells, and what does any one of those coverings look like? With dominoes, which join cells that share a side, the answer is OEIS A004003: a chessboard has 12,988,816 tilings. With domicules, which may also join cells that share only a corner, it is OEIS A239273, and the chessboard has 3,263,262,629,905. Both sequences, counted here up to 20 × 20:
