Domino Tiling

GitHub Blog post Dawid Ryłko

How many ways can you cover a chessboard with 32 dominoes? Exactly 12,988,816 (OEIS A004003). This page lets you browse every one of them, and every tiling of other boards up to 12×12, where there are 53,060,477,521,960,000. Each tiling is rebuilt on demand from its number, so nothing is precomputed or stored.
