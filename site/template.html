Domino Tiling

GitHub Blog post Dawid Ryłko

A chessboard can be covered with 32 dominoes in exactly 12,988,816 ways. A 20×20 board has about 1.27 × 1048 tilings, so listing them one by one is out of the question. The project answers two questions instead: how many tilings a 2n × 2n board has, and what any single one of them looks like.
