## Recommendation: freeze (L), state (L-inf) as a conjecture in the paper

- (L) is not a structural property. Any proof would need the exact symmetry J (item 2: counterexamples at distance eps), but J is not enough (item 6: x >= 3/2). The truth at x = 1 is quantitative: x = 1 sits below a transition x* <= 3/2 where an alternating finite-size mode takes over (at x = 1.25 the two modes are already comparable). The proof routes tried here and in session 2 (RP, moments, TN, LGV, Cauchy-Schwarz, symmetric sectors, cut-and-swap involutions) are all qualitative or uniform in the weights. They cannot see this.
- Paper: keep s >= 0.545343749727 as the theorem and s >= 0.565511911035 as conditional on **(L-inf)** (not (L)), with the evidence above: d_q > 0 for 2q <= 24, a two-mode fit with ratio 0.007, robustness to asymmetry. Also say that (L) fails for x >= 3/2 and for (1, 1-eps). `L-NOTE.md` sec. 2.5 and `REPORT-session2.md` currently claim equal weights x in [0, 100] are safe. That needs correcting, and those files are outside `L/`, so it is left to the paper session.
- Do not continue (L). The only route left is a computer-assisted proof of the strip-free-energy asymptotics, which is a separate project.
